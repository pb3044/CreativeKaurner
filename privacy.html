Privacy

How CreativeKaurner handles information you share with us.

CreativeKaurner (“we”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what we collect when you use creativekaurner.ca or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Email & Instagram: whatever you choose to send when you write to Creativekaurner@gmail.com or message @creativekaurner — for example your name, occasion, and request details.
  • Basic tech data: our host may log standard server requests. We do not run advertising trackers or contact forms on this site.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to inquiries, fulfill custom or claimed pieces, and arrange Island pickup or shipping. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry messages as long as needed to complete your request and for normal business records, then delete or archive them when no longer needed.

Your choices

Email Creativekaurner@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, or to request a correction or deletion. If you no longer want updates, tell us and we will stop using your contact details for that purpose.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. Please do not submit personal information for a child without a parent or guardian.

Updates

We may update this notice as the studio grows. The date below reflects the latest version.
