About CreativeKaurner

CreativeKaurner is a Vancouver Island studio gathering pebbles and shells from local beaches and turning them into small works of art — alongside knit bags, painted fabric, keepsake cards, and coastal prints for people who want something truly Island-made.

Whether you’re shopping for Christmas, Thanksgiving, a wedding favor, or a birthday that should feel personal, every piece is finished by hand with the West Coast in its bones.
